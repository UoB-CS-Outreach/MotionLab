How is the model learning?

You teach it by providing examples and the correct movement names. Python first uses linear interpolation to give every recording 90 equally spaced time points. It then summarises the recording using numbers such as its average, variation and rate of change before finding the most similar examples.

The accuracy score checks the model by hiding one recording at a time and asking it to predict that recording from the others.

“Unknown” is a confidence rule, not a fifth learned movement. When enabled, it replaces predictions whose highest neighbour vote is below 60%.
