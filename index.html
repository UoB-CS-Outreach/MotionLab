Step 3

Train the model

Loading Python

Once you have recorded three examples of at least two movements, train the model and check how well it recognises them.

Your movement names tell the model the correct answer for each training recording. Python gives every recording 90 equally spaced time points, then replaces each signal with summary values such as its average, variation, range and rate of change. The three closest examples vote, with closer examples having more influence. This is the faster option.

Collect three recordings for each of at least two movements.

Accuracy on hidden recordings One complete three-second recording is hidden while the model learns from the others. It compares the hidden recording with the three closest remaining recordings, giving closer matches more influence. This repeats for every recording, and the percentage shows how many were identified correctly. This is called leave-one-recording-out cross-validation.
